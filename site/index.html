About this page

How the levels are decided

Each stock gets points for warning signs. The total sets the level:

  • High (7 or more): locked at its lower circuit, did not trade at all, or the streak ended with several other signs at once.
  • Elevated (4 to 6): the streak ended today, or ended with a heavy-volume reversal.
  • Watch (2 to 3): early or mild signs, such as a streak that ended a few days ago on rising volume.
  • Low: still running with no warning signs, or ended and quiet.

The signs: streak ended today (4), locked at lower circuit (6), no trades in the latest session (3), touched the upper circuit but closed lower (3), volume 3x to 6x the streak's typical day (2 to 3), widened trading range (1), fell 2% or more (1), delivery share fell, NSE only (1), 10% below its post-run peak (1), run gained 100% or more (1), thin trading under Rs 25 lakh a day (1). What this page cannot see
  • The order book. Whether buyers are still waiting at the upper circuit, or sellers are piling up, is the best clue to a coming lock, and the exchanges' end-of-day files do not include it.
  • Overnight news. If a stock opens at its lower circuit after bad news, no end-of-day reading could have shown it.
  • Stocks that did not trade at all. They are missing from the exchange files, so a stock locked with no trades shows up only as having disappeared right after a run.
  • Surveillance listings, price band changes and bulk deals. Check the exchanges for these.

This is information drawn from public exchange files, not investment advice. A warning is a reason to look closer, not a prediction, and a stock with no warning can still fall. Check the official announcements on nseindia.com and bseindia.com before acting on anything here.
